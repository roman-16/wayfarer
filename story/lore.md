# Lore

The source of truth for every quest, book and item text, and the reason behind every rule of the pack. A mechanic without a reason here does not belong in the pack.

## The world

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is the dragon? | The guardian of the End, bound there by the builders of the End cities. It can never leave the End |
| Why one eye, and how could it use it? | To guard more than one world, the dragon gave up one of its own eyes. It was set into the portal between worlds, and through it the dragon watched everything the portal touched |
| Endermen and ender pearls | Endermen carry splinters of that gaze, which is why they hate being looked at. Ender pearls are those splinters; mixed with blaze powder they fly home to the portal. That is why the vanilla eye of ender works at all |
| Why does it hunt settlements? | The Eye sees what stays still. The longer a place is lived in, the brighter it shines in its sight, and cities shine brightest |
| What happened? | The Ancient Cities were the first great civilisation and shone so brightly that the dragon reached through. They drew their power from the Nether and built their own gate to strike back (the portal-shaped frame in every Ancient City), and failed. The sculk is the silence that swallowed them, and the Warden is their last guard |
| Who broke the Eye? | The survivors became the first Wayfarers. They tore the Eye out of the portal, broke it into twelve shards and gave each shard to a people at the far ends of the world. Blind, the dragon faded and the portal went dark. They swore the oath: never settle |
| Who built the strongholds? | The Keepers: Wayfarers who stayed to guard the empty gates, as far from everyone as they could. Their libraries hold the old records |
| Why is there no Nether? | When the Ancient Cities' gate failed, the Keepers sealed the Nether forever, so nobody could repeat it. Obsidian no longer takes a spark |
| Why is the dragon growing stronger now? | People forgot the oath. Villages became towns and towns became capitals, so the world shines again. And the Gazebound, an Illager cult that wants the dragon's sight back to rule through it, are hunting the shards. The greedy king of the Corrupted Eye was their first leader |
| Why put the Eye back together? | The shards are waking and calling to each other. If the Gazebound gather them, the dragon sees again through their hands. The only way to end it: gather all twelve first, open the true gate, and kill the dragon while it is still half-blind |
| Why are the shrines hidden? | The Twelve Peoples hid them so that neither the dragon nor the Gazebound could find them. Only the ember can |
| Why are nights so hard? | At night the dragon's gaze presses hardest. Every fifth night it turns fully towards the world (horde nights), and a blood moon is its Eye reflected in the sky |
| Why do monsters grow stronger? | The longer you carry the ember and the more of the Eye you gather, the clearer the gaze sees you |
| Why is the sky dangerous? | The sky belongs to the dragon, and phantoms are its eyes in the air. In the air the ember flickers and the gaze sees it, and the pull of the shards fades away from the ground |
| Why are gaze tears? | Phantoms weep what they have seen. Brewers call them ghast tears after a creature nobody alive has met |
| Why is a tent safe? | A Wayfarer tent is folded space that is not anywhere, and the gaze cannot see what is not anywhere |
| Why is a shrine safe? | The Twelve Peoples hallowed their halls. No monster is born inside one, and the gaze does not turn its hordes on those who shelter there |

## The player

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who am I? | No one special: a traveller who shared a fire with a dying stranger, the last Wayfarer |
| Why me? | Before he died he passed you the ember, a sliver of the Eye's own light that every Wayfarer has carried since the Eye was broken. The journal he left you is your quest book |
| Why do I feel where the next eye is? | The ember pulls towards the shards it came from. Far away it is a feeling, warmer or colder. Within about 500 blocks the shard answers, and you know exactly where it is |
| Why can't I throw a shard like an eye of ender? | A shard does not want to leave the hand that carries the ember. The ember is the guide |
| Why do I know where the stronghold is? | Once you hold all twelve, the ember remembers where the Eye was torn out: the true gate. First a feeling, then certainty |
| Why do my friends feel the same? | When you share a fire, the ember splits. Joining a team is sharing your fire |
| Why do I respawn at campfires? | The ember is bound to fire. Wherever you light one, it brings you back |
| Why can't I settle? | The ember marks you. Stay still too long and the gaze finds you too |
| How does it end? | When the dragon dies, the ember goes out for those who killed it and they are free. The sky opens, and the last quest is "Build a home" |

## Plotlines

1. **The Last Wayfarer:** the main story, told through the journal (the quest book).
2. **The Twelve Peoples:** each shrine tells the story of the people who guarded its shard, in the lectern book beside its pedestal (`generator/eyes.toml`).
3. **The Keepers:** books in stronghold libraries, explaining why the true gate lies so far out.
4. **The First Gate:** a visit to an Ancient City and the truth about the deep dark.
5. **The Gazebound:** Illager outposts, raids and mansions; their captains carry pages of the cult's plan.

## Quest chapters

1. The Oath: the first tent, the backpack, a campfire camp.
2. The First Eyes: eyes 1-4 on foot; sailing ships unlock.
3. The Sea: eyes 5-6; aircraft unlock.
4. The Far Eyes: eyes 7-12.
5. The Last Road: the far stronghold.
6. The Dragon.
7. Build a home.

Each eye also gets a short lore quest.
