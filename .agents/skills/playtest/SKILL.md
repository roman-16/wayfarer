# Playtest

`just test` proves the pack boots, every shrine generates and the stronghold ring sits at its radius. It cannot judge how anything looks, sounds or feels. That is the user's, in game.

## Hand the user a checklist

The `walkthrough` skill comes first: anything that leaves a player stuck, confused or crashed is found and fixed there, and this checklist holds only what it cannot judge. Pick the items the change touched, and give each as one line: where to go, what to do, what should happen. `just showroom` opens the showroom, where every shrine stands in a row and the compass points at a waymark 750 blocks west of the camp; `/locate structure wayfarer:shrine/<eye>` and `/tp` find the shrines in the natural world next to it. `just play` opens a fresh world in survival, for everything that has to be met the way a player meets it. `python3 scripts/client.py start --state <quest/step> --visible` opens the world at any step of the story, such as `ash/the_door`, with every step before it played. All of them run without shaders, without Distant Horizons (unless started with `far`, which keeps a far view of 1,024 blocks) and at 8 chunks, so shaders, the far view and anything judged by its look in the world are checked in the exported pack: `just pack`, imported into Prism Launcher.

## A blind play

At the bar and at the end of each act, friends who never read `design/` play on the exaroton server with only what the screen shows (`design/production.md`, R3). Nobody who knows the design helps or explains during the play; the designer watches and notes where they hesitate. The `exaroton` skill records the session, and the session record shows where the team stalled and how long each step took. Every stall the screen did not explain is a finding for the `walkthrough` skill's questions; every "this was boring" goes back to the quest's design. The voice joins the plays only in phase 6, once it is recorded (`design/narration.md`).

## Bring the results back

Every number the user wants changed goes to `balance.toml`, or to the mod's config in `pack/config` when it is another mod's number. Worldgen numbers (shrine and stronghold distances, structure spread) only change new chunks: test them in a fresh world.
