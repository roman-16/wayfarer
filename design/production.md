## Open

### R1 The performance budget

The machines the pack is for, the frame rate with the shader on, the server's tick on the exaroton plan, memory, and how long the first world takes to load, each measured in the exported pack. Five shader packs ship (Complementary Reimagined with Euphoria Patches, set in `iris.properties`, and Bliss, Photon, Solas and MakeUp Ultra Fast) and the pack's looks are judged only under the first; to decide which of the other four stays as a fallback for weaker machines while the rest go.

### R2 Updates and saves

Before release: how a running world takes a new version without losing its story, places or things; how players' machines and the server get it; and what a version number promises.

### R3 Blind players and the session record

Who plays the bar and each act blind, and how they are watched without help. The record: the quest engine writing each step's start and end, deaths, retries and long idle stretches into the server log the `exaroton` skill keeps, and a report of where a team stalled and how long each step took.

### R4 Languages

English only, or German too, and when.

### R5 Release and credits

Private, or published on Modrinth; and how the pack credits the mods, sounds, music and voices it ships (`pack/CREDITS.md` holds the music today).
