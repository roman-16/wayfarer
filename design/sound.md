# Sound

The music and sound effects the pack ships: what plays where, and where it comes from. Built in phase 6 alongside the narration (`narration.md`), or earlier where a slice needs it.

## Settled

### Music for the story's moments

Music plays at points the story chooses, not only by place and weather: strings when the caravan breaks, something lighter on a good night at the fire. Classical pieces and violins carry the tragic ones.

**Why:** A loss the player has watched coming lands hardest with music under it, and the road's quiet makes the moments that have music stand out.

### Free to use

Every sound effect and piece of music the pack ships is free to use in it.

**Why:** The pack is shared and played on servers, so nothing in it may need a licence bought or asked for.

## Open

### A1 Where it comes from

Which sources, and under which licences, such as Freesound and OpenGameArt for effects and public-domain classical recordings for music; none is checked yet. A classical piece is free only when its recording is too.

### A2 Which moments

Which story moments get music and which piece, settled with `story.md` once the story is final.

### A3 How it plays

How a piece starts at a story moment: the pack's Reactive Music picks songs by situation, and either it takes story events or the Wayfarer mod plays them itself.
