# Design

What Wayfarer is becoming, decided here before anything is built. Each document keeps its **Settled** decisions, every one with its reason, apart from its **Open** questions, numbered so an answer can name them. Sessions run through the `design` skill.

## Now

**Phase 1, Vision.** Next: **D2 The loops** in `vision.md`.

## Phases

| Phase | Produces | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Vision | `vision.md`: pillars, the player's fantasy, the loops, the twist, the ending, scope, references, what is out | Two sentences say what the game is and what the player does each hour |
| 2 Story and world | `story.md`, and `lore.md` rewritten from it: acts, beats, characters, factions, regions, every main quest with its place, verb and gate, side quests as one line each | Every main quest has a place, a verb and a turn |
| 3 Systems | `systems.md`: progression and gates, the quest log, road encounters, dialogue, the voice on the road, a verdict on every vanilla system and every Wayfarer mechanic, and a spike proving each new system can be built | Every mechanic has a reason in the lore and a spike that works |
| 4 Vertical slice | Act 1 playable end to end, every new system at its minimum | Roman played it and it was not boring |
| 5 Production | The other acts, one at a time: detail, build, playtest | Every act is in |
| 6 Polish | Balance, and the narration (`narration.md`) once the story is final | |

## Documents

| Document | Holds | State |
|---|---|---|
| `lore.md` | What is true in the world as the pack ships; phase 2 rewrites it, and until then it stays true to the shipped pack | Canon of the shipped pack |
| `narration.md` | Who reads which book aloud, with which voice, and why | Waits for phase 6 |
| `story.md` | The story, act by act | Starts in phase 2 |
| `systems.md` | Every mechanic and why the world has it | Starts in phase 3 |
| `vision.md` | The pillars, the loops, the twist, the ending, the scope | In progress |
