# Design

What Wayfarer is becoming, decided here before anything is built. Each document keeps its **Settled** decisions, every one with its reason, apart from its **Open** questions, numbered so an answer can name them. Sessions run through the `design` skill.

## Now

**Phase 4, Vertical slice.** The prologue and 1.1 at shipping quality in sound, music, the interface and the front door, within the performance budget; then the rest of Act 1 built to that bar, quest by quest as `slice.md` plans it, from `story.md` and `systems.md`. Step 1, the world, is built and waits for the user's walk through it; M26 (how often the other owners' places stand) is open in `systems.md`. Step 2, the prologue (`story.md`, S23), is built; the user's first play on a server stalled at the first evening and crashed at dusk, and those findings are fixed, with I2 to I4 (the rest of the HUD, the journal screen, conversations) open in `interface.md` and M33 (room for the team's tents at the caravan's fire) and M17 (SeedGen on a server) in `systems.md`. Step 3, 1.1 The Pull and the caravan's day, is built and waits for the user's play. Step 4 is the bar: the prologue and 1.1 as they ship, voice aside, played blind by friends.

Next: **the user plays steps 1 to 3 and the bar** in the exported pack. The bar is built from what its design sessions settled (`interface.md`, `style.md`, `sound.md`, `production.md` R1): the stamp on every screen, the front door (I6), the graphics Preset and the options (R1, I7); the play record (R3) is built once the user says they will play. Step 5, 1.2 His First Camp with the first memory, is built with S26's change to 1.1 and waits for the user's play; Step 6, 1.3 The Deserter (S28), is built too and waits for the user's play; step 7, 1.4 The Marsh Mothers, needs its step-by-step design session first. N1 and N2 wait for phase 6; R2, R4 and R5 come before release.

## Phases

| Phase | Produces | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Vision | `vision.md`: pillars, the player's fantasy, the loops, the twist, the ending, scope, references, what is out | Two sentences say what the game is and what the player does each hour |
| 2 Story and world | `story.md`, and `lore.md` rewritten from it: acts, beats, characters, factions, regions, every main and side quest with its place, verb, gate and turn | Every main and side quest has a place, a verb and a turn |
| 3 Systems | `systems.md`: progression and gates, the quest log, road encounters, dialogue, the voice on the road, a verdict on every vanilla system and every Wayfarer mechanic, and a spike proving each new system can be built | Every mechanic has a reason in the lore and a spike that works |
| 4 Vertical slice | The prologue and 1.1 at shipping quality in sound, music, interface and the front door, within the performance budget; then the rest of Act 1 built to that bar | Friends who never read `design/` played Act 1 blind, the session record shows no stall the screen did not explain, and it was not boring |
| 5 Production | The other acts, one at a time, every quest built to the bar | Every act is in, each played blind |
| 6 Polish | Balance across the whole road, the mix across acts, then the voice for everything (`narration.md`), recorded once from the final texts, and the release (`production.md`) | |

## Documents

| Document | Holds | State |
|---|---|---|
| `interface.md` | The HUD, the pack's screens and the front door: everything read off the screen outside the world | Settled |
| `lore.md` | What is true in the world, written from `story.md`; the pack's texts are written from it | Canon |
| `mods.md` | Mods, resource packs and data packs weighed for the pack, each with a verdict, and what the pack could build itself | Picked: every Use goes in with phase 4 |
| `narration.md` | Who reads which book and speaks which line aloud, with which voice, and why | Waits for phase 6 |
| `production.md` | How the pack reaches its players and keeps up with them: the performance budget, updates and saves, blind players, languages, the release | Open |
| `slice.md` | How phase 4 builds Act 1, quest by quest, and each step's state | In progress |
| `sound.md` | Everything the player hears but the voice: what plays where, and where it comes from | Settled; each quest adds its moments |
| `story.md` | The story, act by act | Settled |
| `style.md` | How everything looks: the stamp every screen and the HUD wear, and how the world's blocks, items, creatures, skins and structures are made | Settled |
| `systems.md` | Every mechanic, why the world has it, and the spike that proves it | Settled |
| `tooling.md` | How the pack is seen, tried and built, by coding agents: the worlds, the commands, the tools | Settled |
| `vision.md` | The pillars, the loops, the twist, the ending, the scope | Settled |
