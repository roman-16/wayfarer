# The slice

How phase 4 builds Act 1: quest by quest, each step ending in something Roman plays in the exported pack (`just pack`, imported into Prism Launcher). A system grows only as far as the quest in hand needs, built once as the Wayfarer mod's real code, and its spike in `mod/src/dev/java/io/github/roman16/wayfarer/spike/` is deleted when it lands. Each quest's detail is designed just before it is built, after playing the ones before it (S16): the quest's steps in `story.md` through the `design` skill, its texts through `lore`, its looks through `art`, then the build, the driver, the gates and the play.
