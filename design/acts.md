# The acts

How phase 5 builds Act 2, Act 3 and the finale: quest by quest, as `slice.md` built Act 1, each step designed step by step in `story.md`, built and played alone and with a teammate before the next. A system grows only as far as the quest in hand needs. The narration is recorded last, in phase 6 (`narration.md`).
